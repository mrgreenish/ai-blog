The [October preview note](/chapters/what-is-happening/2026-10-06-01-mistral-large-4-enters-public-preview) said API access had arrived and the weights had not. Both are now true: Mistral has published the **Mistral Large 4** weights and dropped the preview label from the API. [Announcement](https://mistral.ai/news/mistral-large-4-oss/).

The API price fell at the same time, to **$0.70 input and $2.10 output per million tokens**, with cached input at $0.07. That is roughly half the preview list price of $1.36 / $4.18 and close to the temporary launch rate. [Official pricing](https://docs.mistral.ai/inference/pricing).

The weights come under Mistral's own open-weight license, which the announcement describes as a modified MIT license. Ordinary use, fine-tuning, and commercial deployment are allowed; a company offering the model as a service needs a separate commercial license. Read the terms before you plan a hosted product around it.

Mistral also says the released model improves on the preview, including a higher Terminal-Bench 4.0 score with fewer output tokens. Those are vendor numbers. The [model tools](/chapters/reasoning-vs-fast) now use the new rates; developer benchmark results for Large 4 remain untested.
